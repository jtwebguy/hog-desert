Takeoff

Space releases the parking brake. Hold W to full power and wait for the engines to spool. Track the centerline with A/D. Rotate gently at 115–120 KIAS, gear up once climbing, flaps up above 160.

Landing

Gear down, flaps DN, 130–140 KIAS on final. The PAPI on the left of runway 36 shows two white, two red on a 3° path. Flare, idle, brakes once the nosewheel is down.

Keyboard or gamepad (left stick, right stick X for rudder, triggers for throttle, A brakes, B gear, X flaps, Y speed brakes, bumpers trim).
